ArchiveTuna is a music streaming app for the Archive.org audio collection:
live concert taper recordings, 78 RPM shellac transfers, netlabels, and
general audio.

Everything is local-first: your vault library, playlists, tier lists,
listen history, and offline pins live on your device. No account, no
advertising, no tracking.

Features:
- Search across millions of Archive.org recordings with filters
- Curated discovery shelves and era Time Capsules
- Track radio, offline pinning, sleep timer, 10-band EQ
- Background playback with notification controls
- Share links for albums, songs, and playlists
